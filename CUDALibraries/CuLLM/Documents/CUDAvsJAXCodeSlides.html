Standard attention (either language) needs the full N² matrix — measured to fail outright past a context length. Every Flash implementation, any engine, either language, needs just Q/K/V/O. The algorithm win travels between languages; the engine win doesn't — that's why hand-written CUDA still leads hand-written JAX on the identical algorithm.
